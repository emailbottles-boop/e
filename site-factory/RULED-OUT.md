## Bellingham

Scouted exhaustively: 424 Bellingham Chamber member pages, 1,430 Whatcom Local
listings, the 134-member Fairhaven Association roster, and dinedrinkbellingham.

**Method note that matters:** an empty "website" cell in a Bellingham directory
is **weak evidence** — most Fairhaven businesses with an empty cell do own
domains. Every qualified Bellingham lead was individually search-verified
instead. Do not trust empty cells in this city.
