## Two limits on these licences

**No model or property release.** The licence covers the photographer's
copyright only. Do not caption a stock model as if they were the shop's staff
or a real client, and do not imply anyone shown endorses the business.

**Not a portfolio.** None of these show the actual shop, staff or client
animals. Presenting them under a heading like "our work" or "before and after"
would be misleading. They sit under a neutral "Gallery" heading for exactly
that reason — and should be swapped for the owner's own photos the moment a
deal is signed.
